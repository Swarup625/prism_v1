the architecture

The PRISM 5-Stage Pipeline

How raw ball-by-ball cricket state vectors are audited, diagnosed, corrected, and verified.

Stage 1: Detect (Baseline Probability Anchor)

Predictive Layer

Calibrated machine learning models evaluate the state vector to produce an initial win probability anchor. PRISM supports model-swapping between Random Forest, HistGradientBoosting, and Calibrated Logistic Regression.

Confidence: Max probability margin above 0.5
Entropy: Shannon predictive entropy (bits)
Calibration: Isotonic & Platt probability anchors

Stage 2: Diagnose (Reliability Signals & Meta-Detector)

Meta-Learning Layer

A secondary gradient-boosted error detector evaluates out-of-fold reliability signals to predict $P(\text{Base Model is Wrong})$. When risk score surpasses threshold ($\tau_{\text{risk}} > 0.60$), the prediction is marked as vulnerable.

Signal Vector: Entropy, margin, calibration error
Training: Leakage-free out-of-fold folds
Safety Gate: High risk triggers active correction

Stage 3: Correct (Manifold Retrieval & Candidate Generator)

Retrieval Layer

Queries an indexed manifold of over 38,000 historical IPL match states to find the $k$-nearest neighbors with matching required rates, wickets, and phase dynamics, computing similarity-weighted outcome distributions.

Index: KD-Tree / Ball-Tree Euclidean metric
Weights: Normalized exponential similarity decay
Candidates: Model alternatives & retrieval blend

Stages 4 & 5: Verify & Selective Decision Policy

Harm Gate & Output

Proposed corrections must clear statistical harm-reduction gates before overriding the baseline. Output conforms to a principled tri-state selective classification policy: ACCEPT, CORRECT, or ABSTAIN.

Accept: Risk < 0.60, baseline retained
Correct: Risk > 0.60 and neighbor consensus > 70%
Abstain: Conflicting evidence, precision preserved

system blueprint

Modular Architecture

Component interactions across the data, inference, detection, and decision layers.

A

Data Ingestion & Feature Engineering

parse_cricsheet.py → build_states.py. 8,665 state snapshots parsed with strict match-level cross-validation.

B

Multi-Model Baseline Inference

train_baselines.py. Random Forest, HistGradientBoosting, and Calibrated Logistic Regression models produce continuous probability distributions.

C

Reliability Signal Extraction & Error Detector

reliability_signals.py → error_detector.py. Measures entropy, confidence margin, and flags anomalous predictions.

D

Historical Manifold Retrieval & Tri-State Decision

similarity_corrector.py + verifier.py. Applies multi-evidence checks and triggers ACCEPT / CORRECT / ABSTAIN.
