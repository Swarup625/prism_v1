System Architecture

Inside the 5 PRISM Stages

Every match state flows through a rigorous multi-tier audit to ensure that predictions are not merely outputs of a black-box model, but validated, risk-assessed, and safely self-corrected.

1. Baseline & Signals

Predictive Layer

A suite of calibrated models (Logistic Regression, Random Forest, HistGradientBoosting) computes initial win probabilities for the chasing side. Out-of-fold calibration ensures reliable initial probabilities.

  • Shannon Entropy & Predictive Margin
  • Isotonic & Platt Probability Calibration
  • Feature vector with 50+ match dynamics

2. Detection & Diagnosis

Meta-Learning Layer

A dedicated Meta-Detector (Gradient Boosted Tree) predicts whether the baseline prediction is erroneous. It consumes reliability signals rather than raw match data, isolating systemic model blindspots.

  • Out-of-fold risk scoring meta-model
  • Model disagreement & uncertainty indices
  • High-risk threshold trigger (> 0.60)

3. Correction & Verification

Safety & Decision Layer

When risk is flagged, KNN retrieves k-nearest historical IPL match states in the manifold. Proposed corrections undergo a multi-evidence verification audit before any change is accepted.

  • KD-tree ball-tree historical manifold retrieval
  • Harm reduction check prevents harmful edits
  • Principled selective abstention when ambiguous

Technical Blueprint

Modular System Architecture

How data flows through the PRISM ecosystem from raw Cricsheet YAML to verified inference.

A

Data Ingestion & Feature Engineering

parse_cricsheet.py → build_states.py → build_features.py. Generates match-level split snapshots without intra-match temporal leakage.

B

Base Model Training & Reliability Signal Extraction

train.py + reliability_signals.py. Computes confidence, Shannon entropy, ECE calibration error, and model disagreement vectors.

C

Error Risk Detection Meta-Classifier

train_detector.py → predict_risk.py. Flags predictions whose signal pattern indicates high probability of baseline error.

D

Retrieval Correction, Multi-Evidence Gate & Decision

similarity_corrector.py + verifier.py → prism.py. Manifold KNN queries historical games, evaluates harm bounds, and outputs ACCEPT / CORRECT / ABSTAIN.
